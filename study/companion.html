Entanglement swapping across a repeater

Node A and Node C have never interacted directly. Each shares an entangled photon pair with the repeater B. A joint Bell-state measurement at B swaps the entanglement onto the A–C link — the mechanism every longer quantum network reduces to.

Proposed quantum internet stack

Layers parallel classical networking, but the physical layer's job is generating and swapping entanglement rather than moving bits — see study/curriculum.md module 2.7 for detail.
